# HippoGrid Architecture Reference

## Overview
HippoGrid is a service-continuity assurance twin for Primary Health Centre (PHC) networks.

## Core Operational Domains
1. **Administrative Master Data**: States, Districts, PHCs, Warehouses.
2. **Clinical Supply & Service Matrix**: Cold Chain Immunization, Emergency Obstetric Care (BEmONC), Trauma Stabilization, NCD Management, Hemodialysis Satellite.
3. **Observability & Sensor Feeds**: Grid power, generator fuel, battery capacity, temperature, road transit travel time, weather shocks.
4. **Predictive Continuity Horizon**: Hours until failure due to multi-dependency breakdown.
5. **Prescriptive Interventions**: Optimization-driven resource dispatch (mobile fuel, staff swaps, cold-chain replenishment).
6. **Governance & Human Decision-Making**: Plan approval, modification rationale, and audit logs.
